# Enhancement One — Software Engineering & Design

Focused on safer releases and operational readiness for the ToDoInTC backend.

> **Formal submission available:** [Download Word document](./narratives/enhancement-one-software-engineering.docx)

## Summary
- Created distinct development and production environments
- Implemented blue-green deployment to enable zero-downtime cutovers
- Added guarded rollout and rollback procedures to reduce risk during updates

## Evidence of Improvement
- Separation of concerns enables validation in non-production before promotion
- Release automation reduces manual error and recovery time
- Operational runbooks and health checks improve reliability during deploys
